### Notes
- **Trademark caution (App Store Guideline 5.2):** naming Google/Amazon/Meta/etc. is common for interview-prep apps and the disclaimer above helps, but Apple occasionally flags third-party trademarks. If review pushes back, swap the named list for "top tech companies" and keep "FAANG / Big Tech" (generic terms).
- **Keywords:** don't repeat words already in the App Name/Subtitle (Apple indexes those separately) — that's why "AI," "interview," and "prep" aren't duplicated in the keyword field.
